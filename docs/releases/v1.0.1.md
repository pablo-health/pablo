# Pablo 1.0.1

**Scheduling comes to Pablo.** Two months after 1.0, this release adds a
full scheduling engine, a two-way Google Calendar integration, client
self-booking links, and the groundwork for patients to sign in on their
own. Underneath, transcription now handles full-length sessions,
note generation is deterministic and scored against a faithfulness
judge, and the audit trail records exactly who did what.

1.0.1 is a large release under a small number. Everything in 1.0 still
works the way it did; what is new is additive and, where it needs
configuration, off until you turn it on.

## Highlights

### Scheduling
- **A real scheduling engine.** Availability rules (working hours,
  blocked days and dates, session length, breaks, slot alignment) are
  evaluated in the clinician's own timezone and enforced on every
  appointment write. Double-booking is rejected at the API, and the
  booking form shows open slots instead of making you guess.
- **Describe your hours in plain language.** "Tuesdays and Thursdays 9
  to 3, not the first week of July" becomes rules you can review before
  saving. The parser has an explicit refusal taxonomy and a 74-case
  eval, so it says when it does not understand rather than guessing.
- **Recurring appointments.** Weekly and biweekly series, with edits
  and cancellations that target one occurrence or the whole series.
- **Appointment requests.** An appointment can be *requested* as well as
  booked, holding the slot until it is confirmed or lapses.
- **Practical details.** A searchable patient picker, a preferred video
  platform per clinician, the note type chosen at booking time,
  calendar density presets (Gentle / Balanced / Compact), billing codes
  on a visit, and per-patient rates with appointment-type default fees.
- **An optional working-hours step in onboarding**, so a new practice
  starts with a calendar that already knows its hours.

### Google Calendar
- **Two-way sync.** Appointments push to Google Calendar; inbound
  changes are recorded as drift for review rather than silently
  overwriting the chart.
- **A four-step setup wizard.** Connect, scan an existing calendar,
  review what Pablo proposes as your practice, import. Therapists choose
  how pushed events are titled.
- The integration is provider-agnostic under the hood and is gated by a
  per-deployment toggle; it requires a Google OAuth client configured
  for your deployment.

### Client self-booking (preview)
- **Public booking links.** Create a link from Settings and clients can
  book against your real availability without an account. Links have
  their own rate limits, a pluggable CAPTCHA gate, soft-delete so a
  released slug stays claimed, and are refused automatically when a
  practice cannot accept bookings.
- **Bookings are held for email confirmation** before they land on the
  calendar, and a lapsed hold can be revived.
- *Preview status:* the confirmation email links to a page that ships in
  the next release, and email delivery requires an SMTP backend
  configured for your deployment. Until both are in place, treat booking
  links as something to set up rather than something to publish.

### Transcription and notes
- **Full-length sessions.** Recordings are uploaded as a stream and
  submitted off the request thread, each channel is sent whole, and
  mid-session segmented uploads are accepted. Polling tolerates
  transient provider errors, and a transient model failure retries
  instead of failing the session.
- **Speaker diarization** on speaker-labelled channels, and a
  per-deployment choice of speech model.
- **Deterministic note generation.** SOAP generation runs at
  temperature 0 and the real pipeline is scored against the
  faithfulness judge in the evals suite; source attribution no longer
  returns zero grounded claims on valid transcripts.
- Note types can override the generation system prompt; dictated
  standalone notes generate off the request thread; clinical observation
  affect is captured as structured congruence / range / stability
  selects.
- Per-account control over whether audio recording is on at all.

### Patients as first-class principals (groundwork)
- Patients now have their own authenticated principal, distinct from
  clinicians and from automated jobs. Every audited action records what
  kind of principal performed it, a patient's own actions are audited,
  and automated or operator access is no longer recorded as clinician
  activity. No patient-facing routes ship in this release; this is the
  foundation for them.

### Security review
A code-level security and HIPAA review of the engine and the companion
apps was completed on September 1. Nothing was reachable by an
anonymous internet user, and no hosted deployment was affected. The
fixes ship in this release; the ones marked *landing* are merging as
this note is written and the list is finalized when the tag is cut.

Merged:
- Test-identity self-signup is off unless a deployment opts in. It was
  previously armed on any install without a production project id.
- The email-action page validates its continue link, closing an open
  redirect.

Landing:
- Delete each channel's staged speech derivative once its transcript is
  merged, and sweep any leftovers in the hard purge.
- Delete transcripts from the transcription vendor after they are
  merged, so nothing outlives the session on the vendor side.
- Serve the password-reset page with the standard security headers.
- Clear the client query cache on sign-out, so a shared workstation
  never shows the previous clinician's data before refetch.
- Clamp the development auth bypass to non-production builds.
- Use a per-request nonce for inline scripts instead of `unsafe-inline`.
- Keep the fixture data module out of the production bundle.
- Validate the production posture at startup (debug mode, MFA, armed
  bypasses) and refuse to boot misconfigured.
- Report tenant exports honestly as "records visible to this admin",
  with visible and total counts, instead of claiming the whole schema.
- Treat prior EHR-navigation results as untrusted input and bound what
  the model may fill or navigate to.
- Verify the SMTP server certificate and bound the connection.
- Strip console output from production builds and stop logging whole
  error objects; ignore the `apiKey` query parameter on the email-action
  page.
- Bind the dev database to loopback, drop the stale Dockerfile, and
  ignore key files by pattern.
- Clear the open dependency advisories in the frontend and functions
  lockfiles.

Companion app fixes from the same review ship in the companion's own
releases.

### Sessions and access
- The server idle clock is the single source of truth for session
  expiry; the idle dialog recovers once a session expires and returns
  you to where the timeout found you.
- Firebase Admin credentials via Workload Identity Federation, with no
  long-lived keys; login works on hosts without the GCP metadata server.
- Passkey sign-in on the companion sign-in page, and a finished sign-in
  tab after a custom-scheme handoff.
- Per-user rate limits on chat and audio upload, namespaced limiter
  keys, non-audio uploads rejected at the boundary, and a dedicated
  signed-URL lifetime for session audio.
- Dependency advisories patched across backend and frontend, with safe
  Dependabot updates now merging automatically.

### Self-hosting and operations
- **S3 as a file-storage provider** alongside GCS, including compliance
  storage.
- Document AI project and processor are derived instead of configured.
- A read-only deployment mode for winding an account down without
  locking it out, and a warning at startup when `APP_URL` still points
  at localhost.
- Deployments can name additional public routes, point the companion
  download button elsewhere, and publish handoff links on a separate
  host.
- Rollouts skip migrations while the database is parked.
- The pentest harness gained a structured domain context pack, a
  narrative ledger with endpoint attestation, and load-balancer-only
  ingress support.

## Upgrading

Images for this release: `ghcr.io/pablo-health/backend:v1.0.1` and
`ghcr.io/pablo-health/frontend:v1.0.1` (also tagged `:latest`).
Database migrations run automatically on deploy.

Two settings are worth checking after upgrading:

- If your deployment relied on test-identity self-signup, it is now off
  by default and must be enabled explicitly.
- Google Calendar sync and public booking links are off until you set
  their deployment toggles.
