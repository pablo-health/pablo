# Pablo 2.0.0

**Pablo now runs a practice, not just its notes, and installs with one
command.** Since 1.0, the open-source engine has grown scheduling, billing,
an inbox, chart features for prescribers, and a much richer documentation
workflow. Self-hosting moves to a single VM in your own Google Cloud account:
`./install.sh` from Cloud Shell, about 15 minutes, about $20 a month.

2.0 is a major version because upgrading a 1.0 install takes deliberate steps
(see [Upgrading from 1.0](#upgrading-from-10)).

## Highlights

Everything here works on a plain install. Features that need an account or
service of your own are listed under [Connect to turn on](#connect-to-turn-on).

### Notes and documentation
- **Note types you shape**: build one from sample notes or a description,
  preview a draft, or adjust a built-in type instead of copying it.
- **Sign, lock and amend**: signed notes lock; add an addendum, or unlock to
  correct an error. PDF export for any note type.
- **Psychotherapy notes** readable only by their author and left out of
  exports by default.
- **For prescribers**: drafts written against the medication list, E/M level
  computed from the MDM choices, and MDM reviewed beside the note.
- **A chart that keeps up**: problem list and allergies with history;
  medication and chart changes proposed from each note and decided when you
  sign.
- **More from each session**: dictate after recording stops, visit times and
  confirmed psychotherapy minutes, redraft after editing details, homework
  and follow-up tasks suggested from what you said.

### AI consent and recording
- Ask clients about AI-assisted notes with a read-aloud script, record the
  answer on the chart, and recording is blocked for anyone who declined.
- Delete session audio once the note is signed, or keep it for the number of
  days you choose.

### Scheduling
- **Your hours in plain language** ("Monday to Thursday, 9 to 5, no
  Fridays"), a working-hours grid, blocked time and limits.
- **Appointment types** with their own length and booking window, an
  open-slot picker, and double bookings refused.
- **Recurring appointments**, weekly or biweekly, editable one session at a
  time or as a series.
- **Waitlist**, **away dates**, and **coverage**: a colleague gets access to
  your clients for exactly the dates you're away.

### Billing
- **Superbills** for every client, including self-pay.
- **Balances and statements**: printable statements, cash, cheque and
  transfer payments, write-offs, a practice financial report, and one Billing
  tab on the chart.
- **Claims prepared for you**: built from the session, checked before they
  leave, and exported as a CMS-1500 or CSV for a biller. Filing them
  electronically needs a clearinghouse (below).
- A billing setup guide that asks how you're paid today and sets up only what
  that needs.

### Practice
- **Inbox**: one list of everything waiting on you.
- **Compliance tasks**: licences, renewals and anything else with a date.
- **Import from SimplePractice**, including password-protected exports;
  imported notes stay as reference copies.
- **Export** a whole practice or one chart as a self-describing archive.
- Colleagues as practice admins, and "clients" or "patients" as you prefer.

### Security and audit
- Read your own audit log; see who acted on a client's record.
- Every sign-in recorded by the backend, and a server-enforced idle timeout.
- Row-level security on every practice table, and tighter content security
  policy and passkey limits.

### Self-hosting
- **One script, one VM** (`install.sh`): it asks a few questions, shows what
  it will create and what it costs, then sets up the project, sign-in with
  mandatory MFA, Postgres on its own disk, HTTPS, scheduled jobs and the HIPAA
  audit log sink. No console steps for email and password sign-in.
- **Backups you can trust**: continuous to Cloud Storage, daily disk
  snapshots, and a restore checked every month.
- **No key files**: the VM uses its own Google identity.
- **Updates in one command**: `sudo pablo-update v2.x.y`.
- See [deploy/vm/README.md](../../deploy/vm/README.md).

## Connect to turn on

The code is complete; each needs an account or setting of your own.

| Feature | What to connect |
|---|---|
| Session transcription | An AssemblyAI key, under your own BAA with AssemblyAI. The installer asks. |
| Google sign-in and Google Calendar sync | An OAuth client in your project. The installer walks you through it. |
| Online booking links | An email provider (`EMAIL_BACKEND=smtp`, `SMTP_*`): bookings are confirmed by email, so links stay closed without one. |
| Filing claims, eligibility checks, remittances | A clearinghouse account (`CLEARINGHOUSE_API_KEY`, `CLEARINGHOUSE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`). |
| Card payments | A Stripe account (`STRIPE_*`). |
| Zoom or doxy.me | `TELEHEALTH_PROVIDERS_ENABLED` and the provider's keys. |

## Not in the self-hosted build yet

- **Practice website**: publishing works in the engine; serving it from the
  single-VM install, on your own domain, is next.
- **Client portal** (intake forms, consents, messaging, self-booking, refill
  requests): part of Pablo One for now.

## Upgrading from 1.0

1. **Migrations now run as their own step, before the app starts.** The app
   no longer touches the database when it boots. Run
   `python backend/bin/migrate.py` on every upgrade, before starting the new
   version. (The single-VM install does this for you.)
2. **Single-practice mode is retired.** Every deployment runs its own
   practice schema. The migrate step moves a 1.0 install onto it, and refuses
   if any row would become unreadable afterwards.
3. **Removed settings**: `MULTI_TENANCY_ENABLED`, `NLI_MODEL_PATH`,
   `MINICHECK_MODEL_PATH`. Remove them from your environment.
4. **Stricter production checks**: the app refuses to start with
   `DEBUG=true` or `REQUIRE_MFA=false` outside development.
5. **Google Calendar** needs `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ENABLED=true` on the frontend
   and Redis (`USE_REDIS=true`) to connect.
6. **`GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is required** if you use billing or
   telehealth: it now also encrypts the practice tax ID and telehealth
   connections.
7. **Sign-in blocking functions are optional.** Sign-ins are recorded by the
   backend; see `functions/README.md` to unregister them.

These steps are for a 1.0 install on Cloud Run. Moving one onto
the single VM is not automated yet; open an issue if you need it.
