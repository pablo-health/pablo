# Pablo 2.0.0

**Pablo now runs a practice, not just its notes, and installs with one
command.** Since 1.0, the open-source engine has grown scheduling, billing,
an inbox, video sessions, chart features for prescribers, and a much richer
documentation workflow. Self-hosting moves to a single VM in your own Google
Cloud account: `./install.sh` from Cloud Shell, about 15 minutes, about $20
a month.

2.0 is a major version because upgrading a 1.0 install takes deliberate steps
(see [Upgrading from 1.0](#upgrading-from-10)).

## Highlights

Everything here works on a plain install. Features that need an account or
service of your own are listed under [Connect to turn on](#connect-to-turn-on).

### Notes and documentation
- **Note types you shape**: build one from sample notes or a description,
  preview a draft, or adjust a built-in type instead of copying it.
- **Sign, lock and amend**: signed notes lock; add an addendum, or unlock to
  correct an error. PDF export for any note type.
- **Psychotherapy notes** readable only by their author and left out of
  exports by default.
- **For prescribers**: drafts written against the medication list, E/M level
  computed from the MDM choices, and MDM reviewed beside the note. Mark any
  medication as harmful to interrupt, or not; the default comes from a cited
  list.
- **A chart that keeps up**: problem list and allergies with history;
  medication and chart changes proposed from each note and decided when you
  sign.
- **More from each session**: dictate after recording stops, visit times and
  confirmed psychotherapy minutes, redraft after editing details, homework
  and follow-up tasks suggested from what you said.
- **Longer sessions**: raise `TRANSCRIPTION_AUDIO_MAX_BYTES` (the default
  holds about 45 minutes) and large recordings are sent for transcription
  without loading them into memory.
- **Restore a deleted session** within 30 days, from Recently deleted.

### AI consent and recording
- Ask clients about AI-assisted notes with a read-aloud script, record the
  answer on the chart, and recording is blocked for anyone who declined.
- Delete session audio once the note is signed, or keep it for the number of
  days you choose.

### Scheduling
- **Your hours in plain language** ("Monday to Thursday, 9 to 5, no
  Fridays"), a working-hours grid, blocked time and limits.
- **Time that moves**: keep an hour free somewhere inside a window, such as
  lunch between 11:30 and 2, wherever the day leaves room.
- **Appointment types** with their own length and booking window, an
  open-slot picker, and double bookings refused, even when two arrive at
  once.
- **Recurring appointments**, weekly or biweekly, editable one session at a
  time or as a series. Mark a week skipped and the series carries on.
- **A calendar that shows holds**: requested times appear as tentative, with
  when the hold ends. Select several days at once.
- **Waitlist**, **away dates**, and **coverage**: a colleague gets access to
  your clients for exactly the dates you're away. Away replies name the
  colleague only once they have accepted.
- Slots stay correct on daylight-saving change days and on split working
  days.

### Video sessions
Paste a link on any plain install; Meet, Zoom and doxy.me are turned on as
described under [Connect to turn on](#connect-to-turn-on).
- Each video appointment gets its room, and in-person ones never do. Editing
  an appointment adds or removes the room.
- Google Meet becomes your default when you connect Google Calendar, or paste
  your own Zoom room once and every Zoom session uses it.
- Clients get the link when a video session is booked and again when it
  changes. A practice can turn the link off in Settings > Sessions &
  recording > Video sessions.
- Bookings, moves and cancellations from the client portal reach your
  Google Calendar.

### Billing
- **Superbills** for every client, including self-pay.
- **Balances and statements**: printable statements, cash, check and
  transfer payments, write-offs, a practice financial report, and one Billing
  tab on the chart.
- **Claims prepared for you**: built from the session, checked before they
  leave, and exported as a CMS-1500 or CSV for a biller. Filing them
  electronically needs a clearinghouse (below).
- A billing setup guide that asks how you're paid today and sets up only what
  that needs.

### Practice
- **Notes to sign in one list**, along with changes from your Google Calendar
  once it's connected.
- **Client messages with a reply-by time**: set how many working days you
  give yourself to answer, and the Inbox puts overdue messages first.
- **How to work with each client**: times that suit, times that don't, and
  a communication and a practical note, kept on the chart. Adding a client to
  the waitlist starts from their usual times.
- **Compliance tasks**: licenses, renewals and anything else with a date.
- **Import from SimplePractice**, including password-protected exports;
  imported notes stay as reference copies.
- **Export** a whole practice or one chart as a self-describing archive.
- Colleagues as practice admins, and "clients" or "patients" as you prefer.
- A **Book a session** link on your client portal, pointing at one of your
  booking links or at a booking page you already use.

### Security and audit
- Read your own audit log; see who acted on a client's record. Repeated
  views a few seconds apart show as one line.
- Every sign-in recorded once by the backend, with your own address and
  browser, and a server-enforced idle timeout that background refreshes no
  longer hold open.
- An account must verify its email before it can open a practice; Pablo
  asks for it at sign-in.
- Passkeys, or an authenticator app where a passkey can't be made, from the
  first sign-in. Passkey sign-in no longer hangs, and says what to do when it
  gets stuck.
- Row-level security on every practice table, and tighter content security
  policy and passkey limits.
- Rate limits count each client's own address, and an optional ceiling
  covers every API route (`GENERAL_IP_RATE_LIMIT_PER_MIN`).

### Self-hosting
- **One script, one VM** (`install.sh`): it asks a few questions, shows what
  it will create and what it costs, then sets up the project, sign-in with
  mandatory MFA, Postgres on its own disk, HTTPS, scheduled jobs and the HIPAA
  audit log sink. No console steps for email and password sign-in.
- **An address on day one**: your own domain, or a name you choose at
  `pablo.clinic` (`--hosted-name`). Move to your own domain later.
- **Backups you can trust**: encrypted from the first one, continuous to
  Cloud Storage, daily disk snapshots, and a restore checked every month.
  Keep the backup passphrase the installer shows you somewhere outside your
  Google account.
- **An audit log that can't be shortened**: the audit bucket's six-year
  retention is locked at install.
- **After 30 days a deleted client's record, notes, sessions and audio are
  removed** by a nightly job; see [what it removes](../SELF_HOSTING_HIPAA_GUIDE.md#data-retention-and-deletion).
- **No key files**: the VM uses its own Google identity.
- **Updates from the app**: admins see a notice when a new release is out and
  choose **Update now**; Pablo takes a backup first and goes back if the new
  version doesn't come up. You can also update from Cloud Shell; see
  [Updates](../self-hosting/start-here.md#8-updates).
- **Claude on Vertex AI**, chat included, from a project of its own if you
  like (`ANTHROPIC_VERTEX_PROJECT_ID`), with reasoning
  effort set per feature (`AI_EFFORTS`).
- Images for amd64 and arm64.
- Start with [docs/self-hosting/start-here.md](../self-hosting/start-here.md);
  details in [deploy/vm/README.md](../../deploy/vm/README.md).

## Connect to turn on

The code is complete; each needs an account or setting of your own.

| Feature | What to connect |
|---|---|
| Session transcription | An AssemblyAI key, under your own BAA with AssemblyAI. The installer asks. |
| Google sign-in and Google Calendar sync | An OAuth client in your project. The installer walks you through it. |
| Online booking links | An email provider (`EMAIL_BACKEND=smtp`, `SMTP_*`): bookings are confirmed by email, so links stay closed without one. |
| Filing claims, eligibility checks, remittances | A clearinghouse account (`CLEARINGHOUSE_API_KEY`, `CLEARINGHOUSE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`). |
| Card payments | A Stripe account (`STRIPE_*`). |
| Google Meet, Zoom or doxy.me | `TELEHEALTH_PROVIDERS_ENABLED`, listing the ones to offer, and the provider's keys. A personal Zoom room needs only `zoom` in that list. |

## Not in the self-hosted build yet

- **Practice website**: publishing works in the engine; serving it from the
  single-VM install, on your own domain, is next.

## Upgrading from 1.0

1. **Migrations now run as their own step, before the app starts.** The app
   no longer touches the database when it boots. Run
   `python backend/bin/migrate.py` on every upgrade, before starting the new
   version. (The single-VM install does this for you.)
2. **Single-practice mode is retired.** Every deployment runs its own
   practice schema. The migrate step moves a 1.0 install onto it, and refuses
   if any row would become unreadable afterwards.
3. **Removed settings**: `MULTI_TENANCY_ENABLED`, `NLI_MODEL_PATH`,
   `MINICHECK_MODEL_PATH`, `BLOCKING_FUNCTION_SERVICE_ACCOUNT`. Remove them
   from your environment.
4. **Stricter production checks**: the app refuses to start with
   `DEBUG=true` or `REQUIRE_MFA=false` outside development.
5. **Google Calendar** needs `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ENABLED=true` on the frontend
   and Redis (`USE_REDIS=true`) to connect.
6. **`GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is required** if you use billing or
   telehealth: it now also encrypts the practice tax ID and telehealth
   connections.
7. **Unregister the sign-in blocking functions before you upgrade.** 2.0
   records sign-ins in the backend and no longer answers the functions, so
   with them still registered nobody can create an account. In the console:
   Identity Platform > Settings > Triggers, then clear `beforeCreate` and
   `beforeSignIn`. Deleting the deployed functions afterwards is optional.
8. **Forwarded client addresses are trusted only from a listed proxy.** Set
   `TRUSTED_PROXY_IPS` to your proxy (`*` on Cloud Run, which is what the 1.0
   script set). Without it, every client shares one rate-limit budget and the
   audit log shows the proxy's address.
9. **Clients now get the video link** for a video appointment by email.
   A practice can turn this off in Settings > Sessions & recording > Video
   sessions; `TELEHEALTH_INCLUDE_JOIN_LINK_IN_REMINDERS=false` turns it off
   for the whole deployment.
10. **The Cloud Run scripts are gone**: `setup-solo.sh`, `redeploy.sh` and the
    Cloud Run auto-updater. The single VM (`install.sh`) is the supported way
    to self-host.

These steps are for a 1.0 install on Cloud Run. Moving one onto
the single VM is not automated yet; open an issue if you need it.
